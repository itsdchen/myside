## Summary

Three tech mega-caps traded well on RelWideMM2 usday variants through
mid-June, then broke on **the exact same session (2026-06-25)** across two
independent strat configs. Topbook (real-world) micro-structure only
shifted mildly for the three names — notional/day 0.80–1.01×, spreads
0.96–1.09× — but HL-side changed materially and *differently* for each:
NVDA HL notional/day dropped 0.61× (faster than topbook); INTC dropped
0.63× (also faster than topbook); MSFT HL notional/day rose to 1.47×
with 2.46× deeper inside book and 1.83× larger typical trade size even
as its topbook stayed flat. In each case HL diverged from topbook in a
way that hurt our MM: NVDA/INTC took hits from a thinner-quicker HL
market that our strats couldn't compete with; MSFT had a fresh
professional maker arrive. NVDA is the biggest carrier (lifetime +$27k)
and lost −$5.3k in the bad window. Verdict is HL-side regime change on
2026-06-25 — not correlated with topbook, not a config issue, not the
July cancel bug (which was fixed after this break and did nothing to
restore edge). Action: disable all three (applied 2026-07-15).

"HL tracks topbook"; on 2026-06-25 that broke.

## Verdict & evidence

**Verdict: regime (HL-side).** New participant flow arrived on HL for
these three tech mega-caps around 2026-06-25 and reshaped the inside
book in symbol-specific directions. Our strategy was tuned to the
pre-change book.

Evidence:

1. Three symbols peaked on **exactly the same session** (2026-06-25)
   across two **independent** strat variants (gf1/usday,
   gf2/usday_cov_0521). Same date + independent configs → market-side
   event.
2. HL/topbook divergence in the following 3 weeks: NVDA and INTC's HL
   activity dropped harder than their topbook (0.61 vs 0.80 for NVDA
   notional/day); MSFT's HL activity grew even as topbook stayed flat
   (1.47 vs 1.01). HL is telling a different story than the
   underlying market → HL-specific participant change.
3. Strat-side signatures track HL changes: NVDA's activity collapse
   matches HL emptying (fewer takers); MSFT's oversized fills at lower
   fill_rate matches HL adding depth from a passive maker with fast
   hedge flow.
4. HL fingerprint direction differs by symbol (quieter for NVDA/INTC,
   busier for MSFT). Rules out a monolithic "market got busier" or
   "market got quieter" driver. Consistent with different participants
   reacting differently to a shared shock on 2026-06-25.

Ruled out:

- **data** — topbook and HL directions are directionally consistent
  per-symbol; no symbolizer issue.
- **config_change** — same date across two independent variants;
  wouldn't happen if config-side.
- **execution (cancel bug)** — cancel fix was later (2026-07-09) and
  didn't restore edge.
- **signal_decay** — decay would be gradual, not a single-session peak.
- **topbook regime event** — topbook shifts are mild (0.80–1.09 range)
  and can't account for the strat-side collapse. It's the *divergence*
  between HL and topbook that matters, not either alone.
