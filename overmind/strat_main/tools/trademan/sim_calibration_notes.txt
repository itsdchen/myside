SIM LATENCY CALIBRATION NOTES
==============================
Last updated: 2026-02-18

CHOSEN SETTINGS
---------------
  alo_ord_latency = 550ms  (0.55s)
  cxl_ord_latency = 750ms  (0.75s)

These apply across equities and commodities (Hyperliquid gateway).

BACKGROUND
----------
Live RTT measurements (gf0/combined_metals_sv_0205/all, 5 days 20260211-17):
  NewOrd->Ack: p50=506ms  p90=822ms  (84k samples)
  Cancel->Ack: p50=503ms  p90=840ms  (84k samples)

Previous defaults were alo=550ms, cxl=950ms (set via sim_latency_secs or
hardcoded). The cxl=950 was too conservative.

EXPERIMENTS RUN
---------------

1. Metals sim-eval (gf0/combined_metals_sv_0205/all, 5 days)
   Symbols: GOLD (all 5d), COPPER + CL (enabled from 20260216)
   All 5 days DIRTY (usermsg + inherit events)
   Result: Live=+288.30  Sim(orig)=-162.50  diff=450.80
   Cancel fill rate: Live=0.12%  Sim=0.12% (total matched, per-sym diverged)

2. Latency sweep v1: alo={400,450,500,550} x cxl={500,650,800,950}
   Key finding: CXL dominates, ALO has minimal effect.
   cxl=500 all profitable (+399 to +517), cxl>=650 all negative.
   But cxl=500 undershoots cancel fill rate (0.08% vs live 0.12%).

3. Targeted sweep: a550/c750
   Result: sum_pnl=-270.79 vs live +288.30 (diff=559)
   Worse than orig on PNL, but not a fair comparison given all-dirty days.

4. Latency sweep v2: alo={400,450,500,550} x cxl={500,650,750,800}
   Confirmed cxl=500 gives best PNL match but unrealistic CFR.
   Confirmed ALO doesn't matter (identical results across ALO values
   for GOLD-only days; minor variation on multi-symbol days).

ANALYSIS & DECISION
--------------------
- cxl=500 produces PNL closest to live (|diff|=111 for a550c500) but
  undershoots cancel fill rate (0.08% vs 0.12% live). This means sim
  cancels more successfully than live, reducing adverse selection and
  inflating sim PNL. Not a more accurate sim, just a more optimistic one.

- cxl=750 threads the needle between equities and commodities settings,
  keeping latency parameters consistent across strats.

- The large sim-live PNL gap is driven primarily by:
  (a) All days being DIRTY (usermsg/inherit replay inaccuracies)
  (b) CL: 48 live trades vs 1 sim trade on 20260216 (usermsg-driven)
  (c) Inherent limitations of replaying manual interventions in sim

- No evidence of gateway blockage effects at any latency threshold tested.
  PNL improvement is monotonic as cxl decreases, no cliff or step change.

- ALO latency has negligible impact across the tested range (400-550ms).
  Kept at 550ms to match observed p50 RTT.

OPEN ISSUE: FIXED RTT vs LIVE TAIL LATENCY (2026-03-30)
-------------------------------------------------------
Sim uses fixed RTTs (alo=550ms, cxl=750ms) with zero variance. Live RTTs
have significant tail latency that the sim does not model.

Example: CL on gf0/allday_search_20260311, 2026-03-23 07:00-08:00 window
(large price drop, high activity):

                  Live        Sim
  NewOrd->Ack:
    p50           608ms       550ms (fixed)
    p90           850ms       550ms (fixed)
    p99          1338ms       550ms (fixed)
    max          2138ms       550ms (fixed)
  Cancel->Ack:
    p50           616ms       750ms (fixed)
    p90           867ms       750ms (fixed)
    p99          1216ms       750ms (fixed)
    max          2187ms       750ms (fixed)

  Orders sent:    934        1650
  Execs:          111         674
  Fill rate:     11.9%       40.9%  (by count)
  Fill rate:      5.0%        5.5%  (by shs)
  VWAP:         88.93       88.87

The sim's fixed 550ms is near the live median, but:
  1. Sim sends ~1.8x more orders (zero-latency quoting allows faster
     re-entry after cancel/fill cycles).
  2. Sim fill rate by count is 3.4x higher (40.9% vs 11.9%). By shares
     it's similar (5.5% vs 5.0%), suggesting sim fills many small orders
     that live misses due to latency.
  3. During fast-moving sessions, live p90-p99 RTTs (850-1338ms) mean
     orders sit exposed much longer, leading to more adverse selection
     and missed opportunities. The sim never experiences this.

Also observed: sim starts sending orders immediately at session open
(e.g., 18:00:00), while live has a startup delay (~46s on this day).
This lets sim capture early-session fills that live misses entirely.

No solution yet. Possible approaches:
  - Add RTT jitter to sim (e.g., sample from live RTT distribution)
  - Add configurable warmup delay to sim
  - Discount early-session sim PnL in reports
  - Accept the gap and use sim as directional/relative tool only
